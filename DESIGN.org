* The Big Picture

** gitist as Bootstrapper and Maintainer

gitist has two aspects: (1) initial cloning, as part of bringing up a platform; (2) continuous
development on a platform: pull, status, consistency across a gitGroup, integration with Magit.

** Towards the -sbom.pcs Model

The end goal is to cultivate the =-sbom.pcs= model until it approaches the likes of an Ansible
playbook: a platform described as plants of declarative, idempotent items, each with a check, composed
by other plants --- and, being PyCS, runnable locally or remotely (RO) and documented in Blee.

bisos.sbom already has the package side (=pkgsSeed=: =aptPkg=, =pipPkg=, pipx, =npmPkg=, =sbomSpec=
for composing sboms, an =_isInstalled= check per kind) and two empty placeholders for the rest,
=fileSysSeed.py= and =reposSeed.py=. What is missing:
- *repos* --- gitist, merged into sbom while remaining usable on its own (PyCS's =_csu.py= and plant
  framework allow both);
- *fileSys* --- directories, files, symlinks, ownership, modes, ACLs;
- *unix* --- sudo execs and other commands.

With these, the scripts of =bisos-pip/common= (the pbds) and =bisos-pip/bx-bases= are retired: all of
them become =-sbom.pcs= specs, and =raw-bisos.sh= shrinks to what must happen before Python.

* The Raw-BISOS Chain Today

Read 2026-11-15. The bootstrap model is documented in the Blee panel
=blee-binders/bisos-core/bootstrap/bootstrapModel/= and in bxGenesis/start's AI-Activity.org ("The
Chain"); this is the part that matters here.

** The Stages

| Stage | Program                    | Runs from                 | Repo                   |
|-------+----------------------------+---------------------------+------------------------|
|     0 | =raw-bisos.sh=               | =wget= from bxGenesis/start | bxGenesis/start        |
|     1 | =provisionBisos.sh=          | pipx (=bisos.provision=)    | bisos-pip/provision    |
|     2 | =bisosProvisioners.sh= & co. | =/opt/bisosProvisioner/...= | bxGenesis/provisioners |
|     3 | =bsipProvision.sh=           | =/bisos/core/bsip/bin=      | bisos/bsip4            |
|   CBM | =sysCbmManage.cs=            | =/bisos/core/bsip/bin=      | bisos-pip/capability   |

- *0* --- =raw-bisos.sh= is 1,494 lines, almost all Standalone-ICM boilerplate. Its work: sudoers,
  apt sources, =emacs=, =pipx=, =pipx install bisos.provision=, then stage 1.
- *1* --- apt sources again, =git=, clone =bxGenesis/provisioners= into =/opt/bisosProvisioner=.
- *2* --- python for venvs; accounts (=bisosGroupAccount.sh=); =/var/bisos=, =/bisos=, =/bxo=, =/de=
  with owner, mode 2775 and default ACLs; =git config= (=safe.directory=, =url.https://.insteadOf
  git://=); then the pbds, with the pipx-installed =bx-bases= and =bx-gitReposBases=:
  - =bisosRoot=, =bleeRoot=, =deRunRoot=, =bxoRoot= --- directories, symlinks, venv commands;
  - =bxReposCollection=, =bxReposRoot= (203 =gitClone= / =gitCloneBase= lines, by GitHub org),
    =extRepos= --- the first, anonymous clone.
- *3* --- accounts, usage account bases, essentials, python system and venvs, virtualization; then
  =sysBaseGeneralAugment=: currents, the platform and site BPOs, three sboms (=pyVenv-asc=,
  =rawBisos=, =nvm=), =bxRepos-gitist.pcs= (repos missing from the registry), Chrome, Blee.
- *CBM* --- capabilities over the raw platform (=fullyMaterialize=), absorbed separately.

The pbds are already declarative: =pbdDict_bisosRoot= is a list of =directory()=, =symLink()=,
=command()= entries; =pbdDict_bxReposRoot= of =gitClone()= / =gitCloneBase()=. They are written as
Python modules of bisos.common, so every change is a PyPI release before a fresh chain sees it.
=gitCloneBase= clones a gitGroup's base (or =.github=) repo *as* the gitGroup directory --- the
layout README.org's gitGroup Directory replaces.

* The Order of Things

Each step is useful on its own and leaves the chain working.

1. *The model* --- gitGroup, its directory layout, base, gitGroupsSet, membership, server. Done in
   README.org (2026-11-15), except as the open questions below say.
2. *gitist as the repo registry* --- a gitGroupsSet plant in a ByStar repo replaces
   =baseDirBxRepos.py= and =bxRepos-gitist.pcs=; =gitistGroup.cs= clones, pulls and reports over
   gitGroup directories. First, because that is where the pain is.
3. *=reposSeed= in sbom* --- an sbom names gitGroupsSets; =sbom_fullUpdate= clones them through
   gitist.
4. *=fileSysSeed= and =unix=* --- the pbd vocabulary as seeds; the =bisosRoot= etc. pbds and stage 2's
   setup become sboms.
5. *Retire* =bx-bases=, bisos.common's pbds and bxGenesis/provisioners.
6. *Collapse the stages* --- =raw-bisos.sh= does what must precede Python, installs =bisos.provision=
   with pipx, and runs one sbom; everything else is composed by =sbomsList=.
7. *Check and regression* --- "does this platform match the chain" is a read-only walk of the sboms;
   regression on VMs (=bxObjects/bro_dockerfiles=). The CBM layer follows the raw chain, unchanged.

* Step Details

** Step 1: The Model

Settled in README.org: Repo; gitGroup (local name vs. server name); the two special repos, =base= and
=gitGroup= -> =.github=; the gitGroup directory as a plain directory; members; the gitGroup plant,
rules and statuses; cached lists; gitGroupsSet with explicit membership; gitGroupsSetServer;
gitGroup-Repos-Profile; anon and auth.

** Step 2: gitist as the Repo Registry

- *The gitGroupsSet plant* --- in a repo of its own in the =ByStar= organization. Per gitGroup: local
  name, server name (=blee= <- =bx-blee=), its server, and the explicit list of member repos; per
  set: the canonical base (=/bisos/git=).
- *Membership* (decided, "C"): explicit lists when installing; "all but these" when editing. A
  developer command (e.g. =gitGroupsSet_status=) compares each list with the server's API and
  reports repos new on the server, listed repos gone from it, and those skipped by the exclusion
  patterns (=*.moved=, =defunc-*=, =*.obsolete=, archived); an update writes the new lists for review.
  Installs use no API (no rate limit; the same tree every time). This is the gitGroup engine's
  pattern (status, then explicit update) applied to the set.
- *Layout*: =base= and =.github= are cloned as members; =gitGroup= -> =.github= is made; the gitGroup
  directory is a plain directory (no more =gitCloneBase=).
- *=gitistGroup.cs=* --- a plain =.cs= (no plant needed) whose arguments, or stdin, are gitGroup
  directories: =gitistGroup.cs -i pull /bisos/git/bxRepos/bisos-pip=. Git operations (clone the
  missing, pull, fetch, status) work without a plant; rule operations delegate to the gitGroup's
  plant in =<dir>/base/py3/bin/=. A gitGroup is recognized by its =base= and =gitGroup=.
- *anon / auth* --- from the =/bisos/git/bxRepos= symlink, as =bxRepos-gitist.pcs= does today;
  auth over ssh with a host alias (=gitLabel=).
- Then bsip4's =bxRepos-gitist.pcs= and bisos.common's =pbdDict_bxReposRoot= are retired, after a
  fresh chain on a VM clones the same tree. =bxReposCollection= and =extRepos= follow (open).
- Continues bxGenesis/start WorkPlan 2.2 ("Modernization of raw-bisos with gitist"), whose first
  step is =bxRepos-gitist.pcs=.

** Step 3: reposSeed in sbom

=bisos.sbom='s =reposSeed.py= (an empty placeholder today) declares gitGroupsSets (or gitGroups) an
sbom needs; =sbom_fullUpdate= clones them by calling gitist's CSU, and an =_isInstalled= check tells
what is missing. The declaration is sbom's; the work stays gitist's, so gitist stays usable alone.

** Step 4: fileSysSeed and unix

- =fileSysSeed.py= (an empty placeholder today): =directory(path, owner, group, mode, acl)=,
  =symLink(path, target)=, =file(path, content or template)=, each idempotent with a check. The
  vocabulary is the pbds' (=directory=, =symLink=, =command=), made into planted data.
- A =unix= seed: =command(...)= with a check that says whether it is needed, and sudo execution;
  accounts and groups.
- gitist WorkPlan Stages 2 and 3 (=unix_csu=, =sudo_csu=) belong here, in sbom, not in gitist.
- Then =pbdDict_bisosRoot=, =deRunRoot=, =bxoRoot= and stage 2's setup become =-sbom.pcs= files.

** Step 5: Retire bx-bases and bisos.common's pbds

Once steps 2-4 cover them: =bx-bases=, =bx-gitReposBases=, the pbd modules of bisos.common, and
bxGenesis/provisioners (stage 2).

** Step 6: Collapse the Stages

- =raw-bisos.sh= keeps only what must happen before Python: sudoers, apt sources, =pipx=; then
  =pipx install bisos.provision= and one sbom.
- *The bootstrap plants live in =bisos.provision=* (decided): before =/bisos= exists they are run
  from the pipx install. From =/bisos/core/bsip/bin= on (today's stage 3), plants live in bsip4.
- One top sbom (=rawBisos-sbom.pcs= with a real =sbomsList=) composes the rest: packages, venvs,
  repos, fileSys, the platform and site BPOs, Blee. Today's stage-3 script steps become its entries.

** Step 7: Check and Regression

Every seed has a check, so "does this platform match the chain" becomes a read-only walk of the
sboms --- the =pipPkgsStatus= pattern (bisos.pycs) generalized. Regression: =bro_dockerfiles= on VMs
(never =raw-bisos.sh= in a container).

* Facilities: Bootstrap, Post-Clone or Both

Each gitist facility says which it is:

| Facility                              | Kind       | Note                                  |
|---------------------------------------+------------+---------------------------------------|
| =gitist_reposList=, =_clone=, =_reposClone= | bootstrap  | mass cloning, per org                 |
| gitGroupsSet clone (step 2)           | bootstrap  | the registry                          |
| =gitGroupsSet_status= (step 2)          | post-clone | keeps the lists current; uses the API |
| =gitistGroup.cs= (step 2)               | both       | clones the missing; pull, status      |
| =gitGroup_status=, =_update=, =_diff=       | post-clone | needs the base repo's plant           |
| =gitGroup_cachedListUpdate=             | post-clone | Blee's Magit menus                    |

* Known Problems Now

- *The overview repos and the registry* (found 2026-11-15). bisos.common's =pbdDict_bxReposRoot=
  still clones =bisos-pip/overview=, =blee/overview= (=bx-blee/overview=) and =bxObjects/overview=, and
  clones neither =bisos-pip/base= nor =blee/base= as a member (=bisos-pip/= is cloned from =.github=,
  =blee/= from =bx-blee/base=). Deleting the overview repos on GitHub breaks those clone lines on the
  next install, and the bsip4 symlinks to the gitGroup plants dangle there. Interim fix: add
  =bisos-pip/base= to =bxRepos-gitist.pcs=; =gitClone('blee/base', 'bx-blee/base')= in bisos.common
  (gitist does not map =blee= <- =bx-blee= yet); drop the overview lines (a bisos.common release).
- *The gitGroup directory is itself a repo* on current platforms (=gitCloneBase=). Not fixed by hand:
  the next install would recreate it. Fixed by step 2.
- Both items above: the interim fix is bxGenesis/base AI-WorkPlan Stage 2 (2026-11-22; 2.2 in
  bisos.common =pbdDict_bxReposRoot= (=gitGroupDir()=, no overview lines) and =bxRepos-gitist.pcs=;
  uncommitted, a release and a VM run pending) --- step 2's Layout bullet, ahead of the rest of it.
- =anon= clones in the registry use =git://=, which GitHub turned off in 2022; they work through the
  =url.https://.insteadOf git://= that stage 2 sets.

* Open Questions

- Which gitGroups get "all but" exclusion patterns, and which a curated list only (bisos-pip has many
  retired repos).
- The name of the ByStar repo holding the gitGroupsSet, and of its plant.
- Whether =bxReposCollection= (top-level directories and links) and =extRepos= (external repos)
  become part of the gitGroupsSet or fileSys sboms.
- How the gitGroupsSet relates to gitGroup-Repos-Profiles (labels): a platform cloning a subset.
- GitLab gitGroups: the repo that takes =.github='s role.
- The =unix= seed's boundary with =pkgsSeed= (accounts, python for venvs).

* Decisions

- 2026-11-15 --- =bx-gitRepos= stays beside gitist (not obsoleted).
- 2026-11-15 --- The gitGroup engine: rules in each gitGroup's own repo; status read-only, update
  writes only, never stages (WorkPlan 7.1-7.2).
- 2026-11-15 --- =overview= is not a good name; =base= is. Not renamed on GitHub: the files moved into
  the existing =base= repos and =overview= is deleted (7.9). Base repos are not packages: the plants
  are =.spcs= on PATH through bsip4.
- 2026-11-15 --- A gitGroup has two special repos, =base= and =gitGroup= (-> =.github=); the gitGroup
  directory is a plain directory. The parent-as-repo layout is fixed in the chain, not by hand.
- 2026-11-15 --- Bootstrap plants live in =bisos.provision=.
- 2026-11-15 --- The gitGroupsSet lives in a repo of its own in the =ByStar= organization.
- 2026-11-15 --- Membership: explicit lists when installing, "all but" when editing (option C).
- 2026-11-15 --- Order: repos (step 2) before fileSys (step 4).
- 2026-11-02 --- (bxGenesis/start 2.2) Chain plants live in =/bisos/core/bsip/bin= (bsip4), not in a
  package's bin: a change is a push, not a release. Stage-3 plants use =#!/bin/env python= (bisos3).
- 2026-11-02 --- (bxGenesis/start 2.2) Anon then auth: the =/bisos/git/bxRepos= symlink chooses.
